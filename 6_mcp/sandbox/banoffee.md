# Banoffee Pie Recipe

## Ingredients
- 1 1/2 cups (150g) digestive biscuits, crushed
- 1/2 cup (115g) unsalted butter, melted
- 1 can (14 oz/397g) dulce de leche or caramel
- 3 large ripe bananas
- 1 1/2 cups (360ml) heavy cream
- 2 tablespoons powdered sugar
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- Grated chocolate or cocoa powder, for garnish

## Instructions

1. **Prepare the base:**
   - Mix the crushed digestive biscuits with melted butter until combined.
   - Press the mixture firmly into the base of a 9-inch (23cm) springform or pie tin.
   - Chill in the refrigerator for 10-15 minutes to set.

2. **Add caramel:**
   - Spread the dulce de leche or caramel evenly over the biscuit base.

3. **Add bananas:**
   - Slice the bananas and arrange them evenly over the caramel layer.

4. **Make whipped cream:**
   - In a mixing bowl, whip the heavy cream with powdered sugar and vanilla extract until soft peaks form.

5. **Assemble the pie:**
   - Spread the whipped cream over the banana layer.

6. **Garnish and serve:**
   - Sprinkle grated chocolate or cocoa powder on top.
   - Chill for at least 30 minutes before serving.

Enjoy your delicious Banoffee Pie!

---

This recipe offers a quick and easy way to create a classic British dessert with a buttery biscuit base, rich caramel, fresh bananas, and fluffy whipped cream.
